# Games Quick Guides

Egg Shen Bot's games are quick plays you aim at each other in chat: throw food, deal a horror-movie doom, hand someone a potion, or rescue them from one. Every play has an outcome, every outcome moves points, and the points add up to items, prizes, titles and a place on the leaderboard. Now and then a monster attacks the drive-in and the whole server fights it together in a **siege**.

In Discord, **`/how-to-play`** shows the same thing in a short card per topic, with this server's own limits. It's private unless you add `private:false`.

## The basics, in four lines

- **Some games hurt and some help:** `/foodfight throw` and `/foodfight feed`, `/doom fate` and `/rescue attempt`, and `/potion give` with a harmful or a helpful potion. Aim one at a member, a role or @everyone.
- **Every play scores for you,** and for them when they're a member. Good plays in a row build a 🔥 streak.
- **Play enough and the games pay you back:** items for your Glovebox, prizes, titles and a nights-in-a-row bonus.
- **See where you stand** with `/scoreboard`, right-click someone → **Apps** → **Game card**, or `/leaderboard`.
