# Games & Scores

Egg Shen Bot's games are quick plays you aim at each other in chat. Throw a pie, deal a horror-movie fate, hand someone a potion, or rescue them from one. Every play draws a line from hundreds of movie and TV references, every outcome moves points, and the points add up to items, prizes, titles and a place on the leaderboard. Now and then a monster crawls out of the screen and the whole server fights it together.

There's nothing to sign up for. Anyone in the server can play, in any channel the games are allowed in.

::: tip New to the games?
In Discord, `/how-to-play` shows a short card for each topic, with your server's own limits. The [Games Quick Guides](/guides/games/) walk through [playing](/guides/games/play) and [fighting a siege](/guides/games/siege), and the [Games FAQ](/guides/games/faq) answers the usual questions.
:::

## The games

| Game | Command | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🥧 Food Fight | `/foodfight throw`, `/foodfight feed` | Throw food at a member, a role or @everyone, or feed them instead |
| 🪓 Doom | `/doom fate` | Deal a horror-movie fate: a zombie bite, a cursed tape, a killer clown |
| 🛟 Rescue | `/rescue attempt` | Save someone, and shield them from the next doom for an hour |
| 🧪 Potions | `/potion give` | Hand over a helpful potion, or a harmful one |
| 👹 Siege | `/siege attack` | Everyone against a monster, with food, dooms and potions |

Each game that hurts has one that helps, and each outcome scores: a hit is +3 for you and −1 for them, a tasty meal +1 for you and +2 for them. The full table is on [Playing & Points](./games-playing#points).

## A week at the drive-in

Here's how the pieces fit, over one member's week.

**Monday night, a play.** Sam throws a pie at Alex. The line is drawn at random, and the small grey line under it says what happened: a hit, +3 for Sam, −1 for Alex, and Sam's third good play in a row.

<DiscordCard name="game-play" />

**Tuesday, the card.** Sam runs `/scoreboard` (or anyone right-clicks Sam → **Apps** → **Game card**). It has Sam's total and rank, a column for each game, the prizes Sam is close to, the nights Sam has played in a row, what's in Sam's Glovebox, and Sam's titles. [More about the card](./games-card).

<DiscordCard name="game-card" />

**Wednesday, a prize and a title.** Sam's 25th hit pays a 🥧 Golden Pie for the Glovebox, and the 150th all-time hit earns the 🥧 Pie Sniper title, +10. Sam can use the pie on a later throw for a sure hit at double points, and show the title beside their name. [Items, prizes and titles](./games-rewards).

<DiscordCard name="game-play-rewards" />

**Thursday, a happening.** The moon is full over the drive-in: for a day, every doom scores double, both ways, and each one says so with a 🌕×2. Kim's potion comes with a twist too, a few dollars found in the seat cushions. [Twists and happenings](./games-happenings).

<DiscordCard name="game-happening" />

**Sunday, a siege.** A monster attacks a games channel. Everyone gets 8 attacks a night to wear it down, the monster grabs and poisons fighters (free them with `/rescue`, cure them with `/potion`), and once a night a report says who needs help. Beat it within the week and the pot is shared out, with a bonus and loot for the MVP. [Everything about sieges](./games-siege).

<DiscordCard name="siege-arrival" />

**And on the 1st,** the monthly leaderboard starts over, so everyone has a fresh shot, while the all-time numbers keep counting.

## Setting them up

The games work as soon as the bot joins: every channel, the default limits, twists and happenings on, and a monster now and then, each siege a week-long campaign. Most servers change one or two things with `/eggshen-config-games`:

```
/eggshen-config-games channels mode:only add:#games
/eggshen-config-games happenings pace:quick
/eggshen-config commands toggle setting:siege enabled:false
```

Keep the games to one channel, pick a faster [pace](./games-siege#pace) if your server plays in one busy session, or switch a game off. Everything is on [Settings for Admins](./games-settings).
