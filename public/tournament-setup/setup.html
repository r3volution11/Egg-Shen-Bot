1. What's in the tournament

A tournament holds one kind of title. Titles are looked up in TMDB (movies and TV), RAWG (games), BoardGameGeek or Google Books.
A CSV with one title per row, or a JSON file that also holds the settings. Loading a file replaces the titles below; nothing is saved until you press the button at the bottom. Templates: straight bracket CSV · groups CSV · JSON · what the columns mean

    2. Titles

    Leave Group empty everywhere for a straight bracket (2–32 titles). Give titles group letters for a groups tournament (4–12 groups of exactly 4); an empty group is filled in for you. Edit a title or year and press Find matches to search again. Sizes and rules
